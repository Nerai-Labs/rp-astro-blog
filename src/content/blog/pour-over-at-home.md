Pour-over is the simplest way to taste what a roaster meant. This guide walks through the gear, the method and how to fix a cup that tastes off.

## Why pour-over

A pour-over gives you control over every variable: water, grind, time. Roasters like [Blue Bottle Coffee](https://bluebottlecoffee.com/) publish their recipes, and [Counter Culture](https://counterculturecoffee.com/) explains why fresh beans matter.

![A pour-over cone on a scale](../../assets/blog-placeholder-3.jpg)

### Equipment

- A cone, like the [Hario V60](https://www.hario-usa.com/)
- A gooseneck kettle
- A scale that reads to 0.1 g

Grind and bloom
---------------

Grind medium-fine, about like table salt. Pour twice the coffee's weight in water and wait 30 seconds so the gas escapes.

### Grinder

A burr grinder matters more than the cone. The [Baratza Encore](https://baratza.com/) is a common first one.

## Taste and adjust

Sour means under-extracted: grind finer. Bitter means over-extracted: grind coarser.

[Read the full brew guide](https://bluebottlecoffee.com/us/eng/brew-guides)

## Storing beans

Keep beans in an airtight bag away from light, and brew within a month of the roast date.
